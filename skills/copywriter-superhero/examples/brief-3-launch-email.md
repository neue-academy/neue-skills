# Waitlist email — beta opens — 3 angles

### Brief
- **Role framing:** studio lead writing to people who signed up 4 months ago
- **ICP:** waitlist member who signed up in March, has not heard anything since,
  half-forgot what they joined
- **Offer specifics:** private beta opens mid-July, 200 seats, founding rate locked for
  life, 30+ breakdowns live on day one
- **Proof points:** 200 seats, 30 breakdowns, mid-July date, 4 months since signup
- **Guardrails:** no fake scarcity, no countdown timers, no income claims
- **Channel spec:** email, subject under 45 characters, body under 120 words, one link
- **Output variants:** 3 angles, each with subject line and preview text
- **Scoring rubric:** subject earns the open without a trick, body earns one click,
  reminds them what they joined in the first line

---
