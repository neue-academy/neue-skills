# Founding-member waitlist — 3 angles

### Brief
- **Role framing:** the studio lead who built the 30 projects, talking to one designer
- **ICP:** freelance web designer, 2–5 years in, quoting €600–€1,200 per site, loses
  two days per project to scroll and reveal bugs
- **Offer specifics:** 30+ project breakdowns, 20+ tools, production source code,
  private sandbox in mid-July, founding rate locked for life
- **Proof points:** 30 builds, 20 tools, 200 founding seats, mid-July beta date.
  UNKNOWN — assumption: the €600 → €2,400 repricing story is one real member, unnamed
- **Guardrails:** no income guarantees, no named clients without written permission
- **Channel spec:** waitlist landing page, hero plus one body block, 90 words per angle
- **Output variants:** 3 angles, one CTA each
- **Scoring rubric:** on voice, one checkable number per angle, mechanism named, three
  separate arguments

---

## Angle 1 — Logic

### Headline
30 builds, 20 tools, one rate

### Subhead
Reverse-engineering one studio project alone costs about 60 hours. There are 30 of them
inside, already taken apart.

### Body
Each build arrives with the production repo. Same files that went to the client, no
stripped teaching version. You read the commit history, see which decisions got
reverted at 2am, and lift the parts that fit your own work. That is where the 60 hours
goes. Not in watching. In reconstructing what somebody already solved.

### CTA
Lock the founding rate

---

## Angle 2 — Pain

### Headline
You have rebuilt the same hero section four times this year

### Subhead
Different client, same two days lost to spacing, scroll timing, and a reveal that
stutters in Safari.

### Body
Nobody hands over the file that already fixed it. Tutorials stop at the pretty part,
and the studios who got it right are not publishing their repos. So the fourth rebuild
costs what the first one did, and the client pays €900 either way. Every teardown here
ends at a deployed URL, easing values and reduced-motion fallback still in the file.

### CTA
Stop rebuilding from scratch

---

## Angle 3 — Social Proof

### Headline
The first 200 seats went to people who had never deployed anything

### Subhead
Founding members opened the private sandbox in mid-July, weeks before the public tier
existed.

### Body
One of them had been quoting €600 static sites. He rebuilt his own portfolio from the
scroll-timeline teardown, put the live URL at the top of it, and repriced at €2,400
inside the same month. None of that needed new talent. It needed the finished file, and
the reasoning behind every decision in it.

### CTA
Join the founding cohort
