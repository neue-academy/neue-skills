# Lesson description — "Scroll-driven product hero" — 3 angles

### Triage
- **Regime:** short-form marketing
- **Deliverable:** in-app catalogue description, 3 angles, ~70 words each
- **Awareness:** solution-aware — they are already inside the app, browsing for a fix
- **Traffic:** hot
- **Framework:** BAB — solution-aware, transformation is the whole pitch
- **Voice source:** voice-codex-neue-academy.json

### Brief
- **Role framing:** the engineer who shipped this hero for a paying client
- **ICP:** designer inside the app, browsing the catalogue, decides in 8 seconds
- **Offer specifics:** 42-minute breakdown, Blender to WebGL, production repo, GSAP
  ScrollTrigger, 60fps on a 2019 MacBook
- **Proof points:** 42 minutes, 60fps, 2019 hardware target, 4 easing curves
- **Guardrails:** no client name, no revenue claims
- **Channel spec:** catalogue card, 60–80 words per angle, one CTA
- **Output variants:** 3 angles
- **Scoring rubric:** a number in the first line, mechanism named, no category nouns

---

## Angle 1 — Logic

### Headline
42 minutes, Blender to 60fps in the browser

### Subhead
Four easing curves, one scroll timeline, tested on a 2019 MacBook.

### Body
The model leaves Blender at 8MB and reaches the browser at 340KB. You watch the
decimation pass, the bake, and the two compression steps that get it there. Then the
scroll timeline goes on top. Every value is in the repo.

### CTA
Open the breakdown

## Angle 2 — Pain

### Headline
Your scroll hero drops to 22fps the moment it hits a laptop

### Subhead
It looked fine on the studio monitor. The client opened it on a 2019 MacBook.

### Body
Nobody tells you the frame budget goes on geometry you cannot see. This teardown
starts at 22fps and ends at 60, and you see which of the four fixes bought the most
back. The measurement pass is on camera. So is the mistake that cost 14fps.

### CTA
Fix the frame budget

## Angle 3 — Social Proof

### Headline
This is the exact file that shipped to a paying client

### Subhead
Not a teaching version. The production repo, commit history included.

### Body
Two reverts sit in that history. One of them undid a whole day of scroll work, and the
commit message says why. You read the reasoning at the moment it happened, not cleaned
up afterwards for a lesson.
